7
Attaching a Photo or File
1
Tap "Attach File"

Find it in the toolbar above the text box.

2
Choose your file

Your camera roll opens for photos. For documents, choose "Browse" or "Files" to pick from your phone's storage.

3
Add a caption (optional)

Type a description in the text box, then tap the green arrow to send. Teammates tap ↓ Download to save the file.

What you can attach: Photos (JPG, PNG, GIF), documents (Word .docx, PDF, Excel .xlsx, PowerPoint .pptx), and most other files up to 5 MB.

Videos are not supported — they're too large. Share a video link from YouTube, Google Drive, or OneDrive instead.

Large files over 5 MB (big presentations, high-res photos): share a link from Google Drive or OneDrive. Just paste the link as a message and your team can tap it to open.

9
Spreadsheets & Linked Documents

NoteFlow has a built-in spreadsheet editor — create one from scratch, upload a CSV or Excel file, import a Word doc as a note, upload a PDF to view, or link a Google Sheet directly.

📊 Creating a Spreadsheet
1
Tap 📊 in Documents

In the sidebar (or Topics → Documents on mobile), tap the 📊 button next to the Documents label.

2
Give it a name

Type the name in the title bar at the top — like "Price Sheet" or "Inventory."

3
Enter your data

Tap any cell and start typing. Use Tab to move right, Enter to move down.

4
Tap Save

Press the green Save button when done. Your team can open and view it any time.

fx Formula Bar

A formula bar sits above the grid — it shows the selected cell reference (like B7) and the raw formula or value. You can type directly in the bar and press Enter to apply.

Supported formulas: =SUM(B1:B6), =AVERAGE(B1:B6), =MAX(B1:B6), =MIN(B1:B6), =COUNT(B1:B6), =IF(A1>10,"Yes","No"), and basic math like =B2*C2. Always start with = and close all parentheses.
+Row / −Row / +Col / −Col Buttons

These buttons in the formula bar let you add or remove rows and columns — handy on phones where right-click isn't available. Select a cell first, then tap the button to insert or delete next to it.

On desktop you can also right-click any row number or column letter for a context menu with the same options.
Copying a Formula Across Cells

Select a cell with a formula. A small blue square appears at the bottom-right corner of the selection — drag it down or across to copy the formula to other cells. The row and column references update automatically.

↑ Uploading Files to Documents
📊 CSV or Excel (.csv, .xlsx, .xls) — opens directly in the spreadsheet editor.

📑 PDF (up to 5 MB) — stored and viewable full-screen inside NoteFlow.

📄 Word doc (.docx) — the text content is extracted and saved as an editable NoteFlow document. Formatting is simplified but all the words come through.

.doc (old format) — not supported. Open in Word, save as .docx, then upload.
🔗 Linking a Google Sheet, Google Doc, or any URL
1
Tap 🔗 in Documents

The Link Document button is next to the 📊 and ↑ buttons.

2
Name it and paste the URL

Give it a short name and paste in the Google Sheets link, Google Doc link, or any other URL.

3
Tap Add Link →

It appears in Documents with a 🔗 icon. Tapping it opens it full-screen inside NoteFlow.

Google Sheets and Docs: Set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view" before adding. For Word docs and PDFs over 5 MB, upload to Google Drive first, then use the 🔗 Link feature with the Drive link.

Add NoteFlow to Your Home Screen

Adding NoteFlow to your home screen makes it open full-screen like a real app — no browser bar, no distractions.

📱 iPhone
1. Open in Safari 2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow) 3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" 4. Tap Add
🤖 Android
1. Open in Chrome 2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) 3. Tap "Add to Home screen" 4. Tap Add
